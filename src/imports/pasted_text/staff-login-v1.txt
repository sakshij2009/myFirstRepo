**Overall Design Notes:**
- The floating card on the dark green background creates a premium, app-launch feel — like opening a luxury application for the first time
- The green background should feel rich and deep with atmospheric layers — not flat or lifeless. The radial glows, decorative rings, noise texture, and light beam all contribute to depth without cluttering
- The card's dramatic shadow and subtle green glow behind the top edge make it feel truly elevated above the background — floating, not sitting
- The gradient on the Sign In button with the inset highlight gives it a polished, tactile quality — it looks pressable
- The frosted glass logo container with the pulsing ring is the signature decorative element — it draws the eye to the brand identity
- Typography is strictly Poppins for headings and Inter for body — matching the entire Family Forever design system exactly
- The noise texture overlay is the secret ingredient — it adds a premium tactile quality to the green background that separates it from a generic gradient
- All animations use the same cubic-bezier easing (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) — this is a "spring" curve that starts fast and settles gently, feeling natural and premium
- The trust badges are deliberately understated — they communicate security and care without demanding attention
